4. 광고 (Google AdMob)

어디에, 언제

  • 하루 장사가 끝난 결과 화면의 "광고 보고 코인 2배!" 버튼 하나뿐이에요. 앱을 켤 때 나오는 광고, 화면 전체를 갑자기 덮는 광고, 배너 광고는 없어요.
  • 아이가 버튼을 누를 때만 광고가 재생돼요. 끝까지 보면 그날 번 코인을 한 번 더 받아요. 중간에 닫으면 코인만 늘지 않고 불이익은 없어요. 광고를 보지 않아도 게임을 끝까지 할 수 있어요.
  • 버튼을 누르면 바로 보여 줄 수 있도록 앱이 광고 하나를 미리 받아 둘 수 있어요. 이때도 아래 정보가 Google 로 전송돼요.

어린이용 설정

  • 모든 광고 요청을 Google 의 아동 대상 처리(미국 COPPA 아동 대상 태그, 동의 연령 미만 이용자 태그)로 보내요. 그러면 맞춤형(관심 기반) 광고와 리마케팅이 꺼지고, 제3자 광고 추적이 꺼져요.
  • 광고 ID(Android advertising ID)는 보내지 않아요. 앱에서 광고 ID 권한도 없앴어요.
  • 광고 콘텐츠는 모든 연령(G 등급)으로 제한해요. Google Play 가족 정책 자체 인증 광고 SDK(Google Mobile Ads)만 써요.

Google 이 처리할 수 있는 정보

Google 의 공개 안내(Google Mobile Ads SDK 데이터 공개)에 따르면, 광고 SDK 는 다음 정보를 자동으로 수집해 광고 제공·광고 분석·부정 사용 방지에 써요. 전송할 때는 암호화(TLS)돼요.

  • IP 주소 — 기기의 대략적인 지역을 추정하는 데 쓰일 수 있어요.
  • 광고와의 상호작용 — 앱 실행, 누름, 영상 시청 등.
  • 진단 정보 — 앱 실행 시간, 멈춤 비율, 전력 사용 등.
  • 기기 관련 식별자(예: 앱 세트 ID). 광고 ID 는 보내지 않아요.

이 정보는 Google 이 직접 받아 Google 의 정책에 따라 처리하며, Google 은 이를 한국 밖(미국 등)의 서버에서 처리할 수 있어요. 저희가 받는 것은 광고 노출 수·수익 같은 합계 보고서뿐이고, 아이를 알아볼 수 있는 정보는 받지 않아요.

자세히: Google 개인정보처리방침 · Google 서비스를 사용하는 사이트·앱의 정보를 Google 이 사용하는 방법 · AdMob 연령 제한 처리(아동 대상) 안내

At a glance (for parents)

  • We do not collect personal information such as names, emails, photos, or location. There are no accounts, no chat, and no in-app purchases.
  • Game progress is saved only on this device. It is never sent to us.
  • The only ad is one "Watch an ad: 2× coins!" button on the end-of-day results screen. An ad plays only when your child taps it.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob with child-directed settings: no personalized (interest-based) ads, no advertising ID, and only ads rated G (general audiences).
  • To deliver ads and prevent fraud, Google processes some technical information such as the IP address (see section 4).

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Android game app "Self Ice Cream Shop" (셀프 아이스크림 가게) made by {{COMPANY}} ("we"). The browser version of the same game shows no real ads (only an "ad placeholder (test)" window), and its progress is saved only in that browser. The browser version and this page are hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process access logs such as IP addresses (GitHub General Privacy Statement).

2. Personal information we collect: none

  • We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal information from children or parents. The game does not send gameplay data to any server of ours.
  • There are no accounts or logins, no chat, photo or voice features, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photo, or file permissions.

3. Game progress stored only on your device

  • What is saved: coins, stars, items bought in the shop, customer album stamps, settings (language, sound, vibration and so on), and play statistics (such as the number of customers served).
  • This stays in the app's storage on the phone and is never sent over the internet. Android Auto Backup is turned off, so it is not uploaded to the cloud.
  • How to delete it: in the game, Settings → Reset data (asks twice), or uninstall the app (Android Settings → Apps → Self Ice Cream Shop → Storage → Clear data).

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Where and when

  • There is a single "Watch an ad: 2× coins!" button on the results screen at the end of each day. There are no ads when the app starts, no full-screen ads that pop up on their own, and no banner ads.
  • An ad plays only when your child taps the button. Watching to the end gives that day's coins once more. Closing early simply means no extra coins — there is no penalty, and the whole game can be played without ever watching an ad.
  • So that an ad is ready when the button is tapped, the app may download one ad in advance. This also sends the information listed below to Google.

Child-directed settings

  • Every ad request is tagged for Google's child-directed treatment (the COPPA child-directed tag and the under-the-age-of-consent tag). This turns off personalized (interest-based) ads, remarketing, and third-party ad tracking.
  • The Android advertising ID is not sent; the advertising ID permission has been removed from the app.
  • Ad content is limited to the G rating (general audiences). Only a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK (Google Mobile Ads) is used.

Information Google may process

According to Google's public disclosure (Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure), the ads SDK automatically collects the following and uses it for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. It is encrypted in transit (TLS).

  • IP address — may be used to estimate the general location of the device.
  • Interactions — such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic information — such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.
  • Device-related identifiers (for example, the app set ID). The advertising ID is not sent.

Google receives this information directly and handles it under Google's policies, and may process it on servers outside your country (for example, in the United States). We only receive aggregate reports such as the number of ads shown and earnings; we never receive information that identifies a child.

5. Children's privacy

  • This game is made for children aged 5 to 8.
  • We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (and under 14 in Korea), so we do not ask for parental consent. If we ever add a feature that needs personal information, we will first update this policy and obtain verifiable parental consent as required by law (for example, COPPA in the United States and Article 22-2 of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act).
  • COPPA (United States): ad requests are tagged as child-directed so that no behavioral (interest-based) advertising is shown.
  • EEA and UK: ad requests are tagged as coming from users under the age of consent, so ads are not personalized.
  • If we learn that a child's personal information has reached us, we will delete it promptly. Please contact us using the details below.

6. App permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only to load the optional ad. The game itself works without an internet connection.
  • Vibration: a short buzz when serving. It can be turned off in the game's Settings.
  • Normal permissions that the Google ads SDK uses for background tasks (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE). Android does not show a prompt for these.
  • The app does not use location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo, or file permissions.

7. Retention and deletion

  • We hold no personal information.
  • Game progress on the device is deleted immediately by Reset data or by uninstalling the app.
  • Ad-related information processed by Google follows Google's retention policies (Google Privacy Policy).

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. You can erase the on-device progress at any time with Reset data. For information processed by Google, you can use the options described in Google's Privacy Policy, and we are happy to help if you contact us.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version and its effective date on this page. If a change affects what information is processed, we will also mention it in the app's update notes.
